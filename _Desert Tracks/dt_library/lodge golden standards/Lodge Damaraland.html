Lodge Damaraland

Stylish lodge on the Arid Eden Route — rhino tracking, elephants & Himba cultural visits

At Lodge Damaraland every day begins with adventure — track desert-adapted elephants through ancient riverbeds, sip locally crafted gins on the Gin & Geology sunset walk, or set out on full-day rhino tracking for an unforgettable wildlife encounter.

Guests can enjoy traditional hair-braiding, unwind in the tranquil onsite spa, or visit a nearby Himba village for a meaningful cultural experience. Conveniently located near the Petrified Forest and Twyfelfontein, the lodge blends stylish architecture with carefully curated interiors.

Cool off in the pool, relax with a massage using authentic African spa products, then gather around the Boma Fire with a sundowner before dinner in the restaurant. A bar with panoramic views, free Wi-Fi and well-appointed air-conditioned rooms complete the picture.
