Strand Hotel Swakopmund

STO 20 Rate Sheet · 2026–2028

Situated on the historical Mole, Strand Hotel invites guests to experience coastal elegance and Namibian charm. With ocean views, stunning rooms and tailored local experiences, it is perfect for families, couples, and travellers seeking a relaxed seaside escape infused with refined hospitality. Set between the Atlantic Ocean and the Namib Desert, Swakopmund blends German heritage, coastal charm, and striking natural beauty. Strand Hotel enhances the experience with refined oceanfront accommodation, panoramic sea views, and exceptional dining at Brewer & Butcher, Farmhouse Deli, and Ocean Cellar. 125 rooms including standard sea view rooms, luxury family rooms, luxury rooms with sea view, junior suites, and a presidential suite. Nearest airport: Walvis Bay, 45 minutes by road. Twice daily flights to Cape Town and Johannesburg. Email reservations: sales@ol.na

Children Policies

Children of all ages are welcome. They can be accommodated as follows: • NOTE: One Cot can be placed in any Luxury Room and in all Junior, Luxury and Presidential Suites • Luxury rooms can only accommodate 1 child under the age of 10 and a 2nd child in a cot • Junior Suites and Luxury Suites accommodate 2 children up to 10 years and 3rd child in a cot • The Standard room and the Presidential Suite are not suitable for sharing with children • Children between the ages of 11–15 should be booked in their own room or in inter-leading rooms. Charged at 75% of the adult rate CHILDREN SHARING PARENTS IN A LUXURY ROOM a) In Luxury rooms (8 rooms) = 1 child 6 to 10 years on a sleeper couch b) In Luxury rooms (32 rooms) = 1 or 2 children, 3 to 10 years on a sleeper couch CHILDREN SHARING PARENTS JUNIOR OR LUXURY SUITE: a) In Junior Suites (6 suites) = 1 or 2 children, 3 to 10 years on a sleeper couch b) In Luxury Suites (2 suites) = 1 or 2 children, 4 to 10 years on a sleeper couch NOTE: No children 3 to 15 years are accommodated in the Presidential Suite. A Standard inter-leading room is available. CHILDREN IN OWN ROOM INTER-LEADING WITH PARENTS' ROOM/SUITE Inter-leading combinations available: a) 2 x Junior Suite to Luxury = 2 suites/2 rooms b) 2 x Luxury Suite to Standard = 2 suites/2 rooms c) 1 x Presidential Suite to Standard = 1 suite/1 room THIRD PERSON/ADULT IN ROOM: A 3rd adult is any person 15+ years. Additional or inter-leading rooms are available at full adult single or double rate.
