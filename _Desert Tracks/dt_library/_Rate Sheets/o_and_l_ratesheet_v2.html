Strand Hotel Swakopmund

Situated on the historical Mole, Strand Hotel invites guests to experience coastal elegance and Namibian charm. With ocean views, stunning rooms and tailored local experiences, it is perfect for families, couples, and travellers seeking a relaxed seaside escape infused with refined hospitality. Set between the Atlantic Ocean and the Namib Desert, Swakopmund blends German heritage, coastal charm, and striking natural beauty. It’s a destination where adventure meets relaxation. Strand Hotel Swakopmund enhances the experience with refined oceanfront accommodation, panoramic sea views, and exceptional dining at Brewer & Butcher, Farmhouse Deli, and Ocean Cellar, all delivered with warm, personalised hospitality. Strand Hotel is situated at the Mole in the centre of Swakopmund. 125 rooms, including standard sea view rooms, luxury family rooms, luxury rooms with sea view, luxury family rooms, and junior suites, and a presidential suite.

CHILDREN POLICIES

Children of all ages are welcome. They can be accommodated as follows:
• NOTE: One Cot can be placed in any Luxury Room and in all Junior, Luxury and Presidential Suites
• Luxury rooms can only accommodate 1 child under the age of 10 and a 2nd child in a cot
• Junior Suites and Luxury Suites accommodate 2 children up to 10 years and 3rd child in a cot
• The Standard room and the Presidential Suite are not suitable for sharing with children
• Children between the ages of 11 – 15 should be booked in their own room separate from their parents or to/in one of our inter-leading, the inter-leading rooms. These are charged 75% of the adult rate

CHILDREN SHARING PARENTS IN A LUXURY ROOM
a) In Luxury rooms (8 rooms) = 1 child 6 to 10 years on a sleeper couch
b) In Luxury rooms (32 rooms) = 1 or 2 children, 3 to 10 years on a sleeper couch

CHILDREN SHARING PARENTS JUNIOR OR LUXURY SUITE:
a) In Junior Suites (6 suites) = 1 or 2 children, 3 to 10 years on a sleeper couch
b) In Luxury Suites (2 suites) = 1 or 2 children, 4 to 10 years on a sleeper couch
NOTE: No children 3 to 15 years are accommodated in the Presidential Suite as 1 x Standard inter-leading room is available

CHILDREN IN OWN ROOM INTER-LEADING WITH PARENTS’ ROOM/SUITE
Inter-leading combinations are available are:
a) 2 x Junior Suite to Luxury = 2 suites/2 rooms
b) 2 x Luxury Suite to Standard = 2 suites/2 rooms
c) 1 x Presidential Suite to Standard = 1 suite/1 room

THIRD PERSON/ADULT IN ROOM:
NOTE: Given the configurations of all rooms/suites at the Strand. A 3rd adult is any person 15+ years and for these occupants any of the additional or inter-leading rooms are available. Here the full adult single or double rate will apply.
