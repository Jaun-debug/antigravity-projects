Winter Solitude

Namibia's Silent Sanctuary

June marks a transition of profound stillness in Namibia. The autumn rains have cleared, the atmosphere is dust-free, and nights under the stars are spectacularly crisp.

Rather than standard high-season routes, this month we shine a spotlight on three newly cataloged, ultra-exclusive wilderness retreats. Built in complete harmony with their surrounding ancient topographies, they offer isolated luxury and deep connection with the wild.

Private Ridge Retreat

Horizon by Ongava

Perched on a granite ridge within a 30,000-hectare private reserve bordering Etosha, Horizon consists of just three exclusive thatched-roof villas. Settle into vast outdoor decks with private plunge pools, and track wildlife on foot with expert guides.

Damaraland Oasis

Onduli Ridge

Hidden among the south-facing granite boulders of central Damaraland, Onduli features six naturally ventilated luxury suites with panoramic views of the Brandberg. Enjoy desert-adapted elephant tracking and sleep out under the winter stars on custom Star Beds.

Explore Onduli Ridge →
Kaokoland Wilderness

Hoanib Valley Camp

A joint venture with the Giraffe Conservation Foundation, this camp sits inside the towering rocky mountains of remote Kaokoland. Six modern safari tents offer true seclusion. Search for desert-adapted giraffe and connect with local Himba communities.
