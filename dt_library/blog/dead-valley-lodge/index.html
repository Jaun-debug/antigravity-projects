Dead Valley Lodge: Staying Inside the Sesriem Gate

Published 10 October 2026
Blog  /  Dead Valley Lodge
In shortDead Valley Lodge is a Sun Karros lodge inside the Sesriem gates of the Namib-Naukluft Park. Its guests may access the park an hour before sunrise and stay until an hour after sunset, so with an early start you can be at Deadvlei for sunrise.

Dead Valley Lodge is inside the Namib-Naukluft Park, between Sesriem and Elim Dune. It is one of only two lodges whose guests may access the park an hour before sunrise and stay until an hour after sunset.

The lodge opened on 1 July 2019 and has 20 en-suite rooms with air-conditioning. It runs on full board, with a restaurant, a bar and a pool, and offers guided drives to Sossusvlei, Sesriem Canyon and Elim Dune.

What travellers ask

Who runs Dead Valley Lodge?

Dead Valley Lodge is a Sun Karros property.

Is Dead Valley Lodge inside the park?

Yes. It is inside the gates of Sesriem in the Namib-Naukluft Park, between Sesriem and Elim Dune.

Can guests get into the park early?

Yes. Guests may access the park an hour before sunrise and stay until an hour after sunset. Dead Valley Lodge is one of only two lodges with this access.

Can I be at Deadvlei for sunrise from Dead Valley Lodge?

Yes, with an early start, using the lodge's early access to the park.

How many people can share a room?

Two. Every room takes two people, so a family of three or four needs two rooms.

What is included in the rate?

The lodge runs on full board, with a restaurant and bar on site.

What facilities does the lodge have?

The 20 rooms are en suite with air-conditioning. The lodge has a pool, a bar, a restaurant and internet access.

Which activities does the lodge offer?

Morning and afternoon nature drives to Sossusvlei and Sesriem Canyon, and sunset drives to Elim Dune.

How do I get past the 2WD car park to Deadvlei?

Since 1 May 2026 the last stretch beyond the 2WD car park requires a paid transfer arranged locally.
