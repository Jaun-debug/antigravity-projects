Inclusions & Conditions

  • All rates are per person, one way, and include 14% VAT. Rates are quoted in US dollars.
  • Rack rates shown are derived from the supplier’s nett seat rate at the standard 20% margin — Mack Air does not publish a separate rack sheet.
  • Rates exclude all service fees at airstrips.
  • Rates exclude departure tax: USD 30 per person on local flights, USD 35 per person on international departures from Maun and Kasane, and USD 50 per person on international departures from Victoria Falls in 2027.
  • No fuel surcharge is applicable for the 2027 season.
  • Luggage terms and conditions are governed by the Mack Air luggage guidelines — soft bags only, with strict weight limits on light aircraft. Confirm allowances at time of booking.

Conditions

  • Rates are agent nett, quoted in South African Rand (1 Rand = 1 Namibian Dollar), per person per seat, and can be used vice versa. Wilderness publishes no rack rate for these flights.
  • Seat rates may be booked on their own — they no longer have to be booked with Wilderness accommodation. The Desert Rhino Camp to Kulala flight is part of a circuit and carries no charge.
  • Departure taxes are excluded from all seat rates (R50 of the Windhoek International and Eros tax is the Namibia Airports Company security tax).
  • Routes not on the circuit are sole-use charters — ask your Wilderness Travel Designer. Some flights have 2 or 3 stops and guests may change aircraft.
  • Lodge hops: no set times (guests are told in camp the night before); maximum 4 guests, extra seats on request. The 2027 sheet lists no Ongava–Mokuti hop.
  • Swakopmund is often closed by fog; the aircraft then uses Arandis and guests are transferred by road.
  • Luggage: soft bags only, max 20 kg per person including hand luggage, max 30 × 35 × 70 cm. Extra 20 kg: NAD 2,500 per sector if pre-booked. Weights of guests over 100 kg are needed in advance.
  • Rates may change with taxes, navigation and landing fees, fuel prices or other costs outside Wilderness’s control.
