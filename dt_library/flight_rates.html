Inclusions & Conditions

  • All rates are per person, one way, and include 14% VAT. Rates are quoted in US dollars.
  • Rack rates shown are derived from the supplier’s nett seat rate at the standard 20% margin — Mack Air does not publish a separate rack sheet.
  • Rates exclude all service fees at airstrips.
  • Rates exclude departure tax: USD 30 per person on local flights, USD 35 per person on international departures from Maun and Kasane, and USD 50 per person on international departures from Victoria Falls in 2027.
  • No fuel surcharge is applicable for the 2027 season.
  • Luggage terms and conditions are governed by the Mack Air luggage guidelines — soft bags only, with strict weight limits on light aircraft. Confirm allowances at time of booking.
