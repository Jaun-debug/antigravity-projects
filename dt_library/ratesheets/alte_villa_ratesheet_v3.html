Alte Villa

Alte Villa is a small, characterful boutique stay in the heart of Lüderitz, with eight rooms and apartments each named after a place in the surrounding ǂKaras region — Kolmanskoppe, Bogenfels, Itchaboe, Märchental. Heritage German colonial bones, private terraces with mountain views, and the option of self-catering in the Itchaboe apartment.

Property Details & Extras

About the Villa

  • Historic boutique villa in central Lüderitz with eight individually-named rooms and apartments.
  • All rooms are individually styled — colonial heritage, German-Namibian character.
  • Breakfast included in all rates.
  • Itchaboe apartment can be booked self-catering.
