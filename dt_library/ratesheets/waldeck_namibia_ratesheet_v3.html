Waldeck Namibia

Waldeck Namibia is a small, family-run private safari retreat at Aris, just outside Windhoek — exclusive-use, with a maximum of 16 guests across the whole property. The fully-inclusive rate covers 24h butler service, all meals, premium drinks & curated wine, all spa treatments and wellness, daily laundry, daily activities (private game drives, guided walks) and road transfer to / from Windhoek airport. A helicopter pad on site supports private heli transfers (excluded from rate). Minimum 2-night stay; longer recommended.

Property & Inclusions

About Waldeck Namibia

  • Small, family-run private safari retreat at Farm #28, D1463, Aris, Windhoek.
  • Exclusive use — when you book, the whole property is yours (max 16 guests).
  • Approx 35 min from the next private hospital in Windhoek.
  • Helicopter pad on site — private heli transfers available (excluded from rate).

Rate Includes

  • 24h butler service and full-service team.
  • All meals, premium drinks and curated wine selection.
  • All spa treatments, sauna, wellness and fitness area.
  • Daily laundry service.
  • Daily activities — private game drives, guided nature walks and more.
  • Road transfer from / to the airport / Windhoek.

Rate Excludes

  • Private helicopter transfers to the on-site helipad.
  • Imported luxury items.
  • Special dietary imports.

Policies & Information

Payment

  • 25% non-refundable deposit to confirm booking.
  • Remaining 75% due 60 days before arrival.
  • Bookings under 60 days: full payment due immediately.

Cancellation Policy

  • More than 90 days before arrival: 25% non-refundable.
  • 60 – 89 days before arrival: 50% non-refundable.
  • Less than 60 days before arrival: 100% non-refundable.

Festive Season & Easter

  • 100% cancellation fee once confirmed — date changes possible subject to availability.
  • Festive Season: 18 December – 6 January.
  • Easter: 2 weeks before Easter Sunday until 1 week after.

Booking Confirmation

  • Provisional bookings held 14 days, then automatically released.
  • Group reductions: 1 room reduction allowed with 60+ days written notice.
  • Additional room reductions follow cancellation terms.
