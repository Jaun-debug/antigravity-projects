Camp Kwando

Riverside tented chalets, tree houses & cruises on the Kwando

Camp Kwando sits on the banks of the Kwando River in Namibia's Caprivi (Zambezi) strip — tented river chalets perched over the water, off-river tented chalets, tree-house suites and a private campsite. Boat cruises, bird cruises and game drives explore the floodplains, with Mudumu and Bwabwata National Parks within easy reach.
